# Target roles

## Tier 1

- Senior Forward-Deployed Engineer
- AI Solutions Engineer
- AI Platform Product Engineer
- Founding / early technical product engineer for AI infrastructure or automation

## Tier 2

- Senior Technical Product Manager, AI platform or developer infrastructure
- Senior Technical Program Manager, automation/platform/API systems
- Senior Platform Engineer, developer productivity or AI infrastructure
- Infrastructure Architect, automation platform or runtime operations

## Company patterns

Best fit: AI agents, developer tools, enterprise automation, workflow software, data platforms, security/identity, infrastructure, and AI-native operations.

## Deprioritize

- Pure roadmap/ceremony product roles
- Generic frontend/backend roles with no user or system ownership
- Coordinator roles without technical authority
- Research ML roles requiring formal model-training specialization
- Staff/Principal roles whose main requirement is a long organizational leadership record rather than hands-on delivery
